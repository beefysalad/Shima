Release notes.

Features, improvements and fixes, release by release. shima checks for updates in Settings, Updates.

  1. 0.1.7LatestNov 22, 2026

    A camera mirror

    • Camera tab: a live, mirrored preview with Mirror, Switch camera and Close. The camera runs only while the tab is open, so the green light goes off when the island closes or the screen locks.
    • A tidier header: outlined Home, Music and Camera icons sit in the upper left. Home opens Settings and replaces the gear.
    • Camera permission: macOS asks the first time you open the Camera tab. Decline, and the tab links you to Privacy settings.
    • Player: previous, play and next are centred under the track text again.
  2. 0.1.6Nov 22, 2026

    Settings, closer to the design

    • Real logos: the Shima mark in the sidebar, the About page and the menu bar, and Spotify’s own assets in the player.
    • Matched layout: an 880 × 620 dark window with a 200-point sidebar and grouped rows. The equalizer selector moved into Music.
  3. 0.1.5Nov 22, 2026

    A redesigned Settings window

    • Sidebar pages: General (launch at login), Music (Spotify status and the Automation shortcut), Updates and About.
    • Quit: at the bottom of the sidebar, in light and dark appearances.
  4. 0.1.4Nov 22, 2026

    Updates from Settings

    • Check for updates: looks at the latest stable GitHub release when you click. No automatic polling.
    • Update and restart: downloads the release ZIP and verifies its SHA-256 checksum, archive paths, bundle identity, version and code signature before replacing the app.
    • Safe replacement: a failed replacement or a failed launch restores the previous app, and a recovery copy is kept beside Shima.
    • First install: install 0.1.4 manually once; after that, updates can run from Settings.
  5. 0.1.3Nov 22, 2026

    A roomier player

    • Bigger island: a 400 × 200 layout with larger artwork, “Playing on Spotify” or “Paused on Spotify”, and artist and album text.
    • Controls: aligned beneath the progress bar. The collapsed notch is unchanged.
  6. 0.1.2Nov 22, 2026

    A tab header

    • Music tab icon: the open island gets a header strip with the Music icon in the upper left, the first piece of a tab header. Nothing else changed.
  7. 0.1.1Nov 22, 2026

    A smoother opening

    • No sideways sweep: the opening animation settles the panel’s new frame before the spring starts, so it no longer slides out from the old narrow strip.
  8. 0.1.0Nov 22, 2026

    First release

    • Spotify now playing: artwork, title, artist, progress, and previous, play/pause and next.
    • Settings: a small settings window.
